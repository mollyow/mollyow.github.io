summary: "Can people correct misinformation within their own WhatsApp networks? A randomized field experiment in India compared fact-checks from close contacts, local journalists, and a national tipline using messages from participants' actual group conversations. Interviews explored how participants interpreted these corrections. Fact-checks from close contacts improved accuracy relative to the control group; the effects of journalist and tipline corrections were not statistically significant. None of the approaches improved recognition of new misinformation on similar topics. The findings show the promise of peer corrections while suggesting that their benefits may remain specific to the messages corrected."
