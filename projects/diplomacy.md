summary: "What kinds of information help outside organizations gain access to United Nations diplomats? In an email field experiment, we randomly offered diplomats information about either other UN missions or world affairs and measured their interest in participating. Offers about peer missions elicited more interested responses, but the difference was only 3.3 percentage points and was not statistically significant. Response rates were low in both groups. The experiment therefore does not establish that these two kinds of informational offers differ in their ability to secure access to diplomats."
